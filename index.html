IMPORTANT

What this tool cannot prove.

— The earliest result found is not necessarily the original creation date.

— A webpage publication date is not proof of when the image itself was taken.

— No search result does not prove that an image is original or authentic.

— This MVP does not claim to detect AI-generated imagery or pixel-level manipulation.

FAQ

Questions before you investigate?

Does this tell me if a photo is fake?

Not by itself. The current MVP investigates online history and evidence. A later forensic module can separately analyze manipulation or synthetic-generation indicators.

What does “earliest documented appearance” mean?

It means the earliest dated web evidence found in the returned results after bounded date analysis. It is not a guaranteed original upload or creation date.

Can I investigate a claim as well as an image?

Yes. Add the claim or caption you are checking. The report can compare it with the dated web evidence that was found.

Why might a search find nothing?

Search coverage is incomplete. Private pages, deleted content, unindexed pages and images with little web presence may not appear.
